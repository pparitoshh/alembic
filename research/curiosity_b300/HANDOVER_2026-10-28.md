## Goal

Raise the average score of ClusterPilot-4B, mainly the weakest test set (`d1`, called GPU/MPI on the card). The best
model is **ClusterPilot-4B 1.3** (`kdall`, Hugging Face `main`): reviewed leaderboard mean 0.847, Slurm 0.933, Slurm dev
0.856, GPU/MPI 0.752; real Slurm 21/30. A new model is published as 1.4 only if its B300 mean beats 1.3's 0.8665 by
+0.02 with Slurm not lower (the autopilot rule).

## What d1 actually tests (why MPI/NCCL data did not help)

`d1` has 106 questions. By group, ClusterPilot-4B 1.3 against Qwen3-235B (reviewed leaderboard, majority):

| Group | Questions | 1.3 | Qwen3-235B | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slurm requeue and signals (`req`, `req-cand`) | 36 | 0.81 / 0.76 | 0.95 / 0.88 | about 0.13 |
| Held-out cluster docs, Aalto Triton (`dev-aalto`) | 40 | 0.61 | 0.73 | 0.12 |
| Slurm job arrays (`arr`, `arr-cand`) | 30 | 0.90 / 0.90 | 0.95 / 0.93 | small |

There are almost no general MPI or NCCL questions, so the 9,938 MPI/NCCL Q&A (batches v300 and v400) could not move it.
The two gaps to close are exact Slurm requeue/signal details (`--requeue`, `scontrol requeue`, `requeuehold`,
`--signal=B:USR1@60` with `trap`, `--time-min`, `--open-mode`, `SLURM_RESTART_COUNT`) and using the supplied excerpts of
a cluster the model has never seen (Aalto Triton docs are held out of training, indexes and the dataset on purpose).
